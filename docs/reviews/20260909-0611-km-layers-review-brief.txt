Sovereign review request: should tools/km_provenance/km_layers.ml continue to exist?

CONTEXT (verify independently; do not take my word for it).
km_layers.ml is a content-based fractal-layer classifier for the ZK corpus in docs/zk.
It was built to raise the KMP-ENTROPY metric in tools/km_provenance/km_metrics.ml, whose
header stated "68 of 87 ADRs are tagged #fractal-l0, collapsing the layer taxonomy to
~1.3 bits". On 2026-09-09 I established that reading was a MEASUREMENT ARTIFACT:
layer_entropy_bits folded over Km_corpus.layer, which is only the FIRST #fractal-lN tag
in a record. ADRs are multi-label (47 of 80 carry all ten layers; 6 carry as few as two)
and the tag block is written ascending by house style, so 74 of 80 records read as l0.
Measured over all tags the same corpus scores 3.309 bits against an unchanged 2.50 floor,
and tools/km-gate now returns PASS with 0 findings. Method: term-frequency vector over a
121-term vocabulary, cosine similarity against one 0/1 archetype vector per layer,
highest wins; classify/1 returns a single proposed layer plus confidence and margin.

MY FINDINGS (challenge any of these).
F1 The motivating premise is gone: the number it was built to raise was never low.
F2 Even applying every proposal reaches only 2.214 bits, below the 2.50 floor it targeted,
   while rewriting 58 of 96 records, 35 of which the tool itself flags margin < 0.05.
F3 Vocabulary-prevalence bias: Spearman rho between a layer's mean term document-frequency
   across the corpus and how often that layer is proposed is 0.988 (n=10). The classifier
   appears to rank layers by how common their vocabulary happens to be, not by content.
F4 18 of 121 vocabulary terms occur in ZERO ADRs. #fractal-l1 (4/13 dead terms, mean df
   7.3%) and #fractal-l6 (5/12 dead, 10.6%) are proposed 0 times: structurally unable to win.
F5 No IDF/stopword weighting. 'sovereign', 'system' and 'wiki' appear in >=90% of ADRs and
   are weighted equally with rare terms. doc_vector is raw counts, archetype_vector is 0/1.
F6 Substring matching, not tokenisation. 23% of 'sync' matches are inside 'asynchronous';
   17% of 'commit' matches are inside 'commitment'. In a Jujutsu monorepo the remaining
   'commit' hits are overwhelmingly VCS commits, not the L3 transaction sense - a semantic
   false positive substring matching cannot detect at all.
F7 Single-label output for a demonstrably multi-label corpus.
F8 No law suite. km_gate has --rete-selftest, --ev-selftest, --merge-selftest and now
   --metrics-selftest; km_layers has none.
F9 The header claims a differential oracle ("the Mojo kernel computes the same quantity"),
   exposed via km_gate --layer-vectors, but grep finds NO consumer of that subcommand
   anywhere in the repository. The oracle is aspirational, not running.
F10 km_gate --layer-vectors passes classify's result to archetype_vector without guarding
   the None case; List.assoc "" archetypes would raise Not_found, which is not in the
   handler list at the bottom of km_gate.ml.

QUESTIONS.
Q1 Is deletion, retention-with-a-corrected-header (current state), or repair correct?
Q2 If repaired: is a single-label classifier ever the right shape for a multi-label corpus,
   or must the output become a per-layer relevance vector?
Q3 Is F3 a fair characterisation, or is rho=0.988 an artifact of my own method?
Q4 What evidence would justify RETAINING it? Name a concrete acceptance test.
Q5 Anything I have missed, including anything I have overstated.
