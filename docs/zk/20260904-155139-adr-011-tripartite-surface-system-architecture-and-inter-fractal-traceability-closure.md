_Decision record (ADR) — captured in the Zettelkasten as part of the SDLC/SRE loop._

## Context (as-is)

To ensure full system coherence across creation, execution, and self-evolution, the Unified Operational System formalizes its architecture across three co-dependent surfaces: Development Surface, Operational Surface, and Evolutionary Surface.

## Decision (to-be)

Adopt the Tripartite Surface Architecture: isolate the Development Surface (Nix, Dune, Lean 4, Gospel), the Operational Surface (BEAM root supervisor, Wisp :4100, ZigVM :8088), and the Evolutionary Surface (Stan AD/MCMC, Ruliad HashLife, ZK ontology graph) with strict advisory-only gating boundaries.

## Agent reasoning

Strict surface segregation prevents runtime instability: evolutionary self-modification and Bayesian duration learning annotate operational decisions with confidence bounds without ever possessing direct admission authority over the committed HEAD gate.

## Criteria · Architecture

Tripartite Surface Architecture connecting Development (Nix, Gospel, Lean), Operational (BEAM Gleam sup, Wisp :4100, ZigVM :8088), and Evolutionary (Stan AD/MCMC, Ruliad HashLife, ZK PPR) surfaces across the WireGuard dual-host mesh.

## Criteria · Test

100% KPI mapping across all 184 system facets, 9,767 Gleam unit tests passing, all 6 local endpoints and 4 remote Tailnet endpoints returning 200 OK.

## Criteria · Docs

Documented in 2026-09-04-unified-operational-system-design-plan.md (Section 30), 2026-09-04-claude-codex-review-dossier.md (Section 16), and 2026-09-04-source-backed-operational-catalogue.md (Section 8).

## Tradeoffs

Evolutionary learning cannot bypass hard zero-trust gates, requiring formal change orders for any structural parameter updates.

## Alternatives — what else could be done

Allowing evolutionary algorithms to directly mutate operational code was rejected as violating STPA safety invariants and fail-closed zero-trust principles.
