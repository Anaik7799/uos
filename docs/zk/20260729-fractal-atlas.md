## The self-awareness loop

The harness reads its OWN state (git + SQLite + this corpus) through its own
tools — `facts`/`observe` for the ledger, `zk_search`/`zk_query`/`zk_anomalies`
for knowledge, `--zk-temporal` for its history, episodic notes for its recent
actions — and repairs its own graph via `--zk-moc` + healing notes. Currency is
mechanized by the `zigvm-zk-maintain.timer` unit; every slice closure
self-documents. What the organism knows about itself IS this graph.
