The Macro Challenge

The Nordic Healthcare Paradox

Nordic biopharma and medtech leaders generate world-leading clinical and engineering innovation, yet face intense margin compression from three converging macro-forces:

1. Hardware Commoditization

Hospital procurement caps squeeze standalone device margins, requiring an urgent pivot to Device-as-a-Service (DaaS).

2. High-Burn Clinical Trials

Biotechs burn hundreds of millions in R&D while patient recruitment stalls across fragmented European hospital clinics.

3. Regulatory Squeeze

The EU AI Act and EU MDR impose unprecedented algorithmic audit and software compliance burdens.
