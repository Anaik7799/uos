Market Taxonomy

The Four Competitor Archetypes

How incumbent consultancies segment across the Nordic life science corridor

1. Product Engineering Consultancies

Firms: Semcon, Knightec Group, Prevas AB

Heavy mechanical design, plastic tooling, CAD/CAM, and industrial automation. Weak in software clinical trials, GxP algorithmic AI, and hospital HEOR reimbursement economics.

2. Pure-Play RA/QA Specialists

Firms: Qadvis AB, Key2Compliance, Emergo by UL

Deep ISO 13485 and MDR technical file expertise. Highly manual, bill-by-the-hour delivery model. Lack proprietary AI technology or commercial sales portal implementation capability.

3. Full-Service Regional CROs

Firms: LINK Medical Research, Syntagon AB

Protocol design, site monitoring, and bioanalysis. Suffer from slow manual patient recruitment and rising monitoring overhead. No clinical AI software engines.

4. Tier-1 Strategy & Management Houses

Firms: PA Consulting, Sirona Health Solutions

Boardroom strategy, commercial due diligence, and healthcare policy. Extremely expensive (€3,500 - €5,000/day) with near-zero hands-on software/regulatory execution.

Speaker Notes: Understanding these four buckets is vital. In sales conversations, clients often say "we work with Semcon" or "we work with Qadvis." Knowing their archetypes allows us to instantly expose what their current vendor cannot deliver.

Risk Mitigation

Devil's Advocate & Tenth Man Pre-Mortem

Proactively countering alliance failure modes & channel risks

1. Subcontract Margin Squeeze

Risk: Prime engineering contractors demand 35-40% markups, compressing 2S net margins.

Defense: Cap partner markup at 20% by contract and retain exclusive ownership of all software tooling.

2. Non-Compete Covenants

Risk: Primes insert restrictive non-compete clauses prohibiting 2S from pitching other accounts.

Defense: Carve out explicit exclusions for direct software licensing, DaaS consulting, and open masterclasses.

3. Algorithmic IP Exposure

Risk: Engineering partners attempt to reverse-engineer Numantra's HCare360 NLP pipelines.
