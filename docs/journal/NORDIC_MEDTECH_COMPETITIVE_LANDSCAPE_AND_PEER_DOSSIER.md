## 1. Executive Summary & Market Dynamics

The Nordic life sciences consulting market is heavily fragmented between three tiers of providers:
1. **Local Nordic Engineering & QA/RA Boutiques**: Firms like Plantvision, Knightec, Qadvis, and Prevas that grew out of contract engineering, staffing, and local validation. They provide solid tactical compliance but frequently operate on high hourly time-and-materials (T&M) rates and lack deep-tech proprietary software platforms.
2. **Global Specialized Regulatory & CRO Advisory Houses**: Entities such as Emergo by UL, ProPharma Group, and LINK Medical Research. They offer international regulatory registration and clinical operations, but suffer from bureaucratic overhead, rigid pricing, and slow technical file iteration times.
3. **Generalist Strategy Consultancies**: Firms like PA Consulting, McKinsey, and IQVIA. While strong in C-suite branding and commercial strategy, they do not write software lifecycle files (IEC 62304) or design clinical evaluation protocols (MDR Annex XIV) at the engineering level.

### 2S Consulting's Distinct Market Position:
2S Consulting occupies the **Cognitive Operations & Digital QA** sweet spot:
- **Productized Compliance (HCare360)**: Unlike consultancies selling purely human hours, 2S deploys continuous automated quality tracking and audit readiness.
- **AI SaMD & EU AI Act Specialization**: Direct expertise bridging modern machine learning pipelines with high-risk medical device classifications (MDR Rule 11 / EU AI Act Annex III).
- **Speed to Notified Body Sign-off**: Turnkey technical file remediation completed in 90 days vs 12–18 months typical of legacy hourly billers.
- **Founder-Led Senior Immersion**: Direct involvement of senior leaders (Hichem Sellami, Safa Sellami) without junior staff bait-and-switch.
