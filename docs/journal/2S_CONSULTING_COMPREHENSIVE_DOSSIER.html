Executive Summary

2S Global Consulting (2S Consulting) is an elite, Swiss-headquartered boutique management consulting and deep-tech transformation firm specializing in Cognitive Operations, Enterprise Data Strategy, Hyperautomation, and AI-enabled Performance Transformation. In the Nordic region (Sweden, Denmark, Norway, Finland), commercial representation, prime contracting, and local client distribution are exclusively led by Bountytek AB (Sweden).

Founded by former Deloitte Consulting US senior leaders and graduates of India's premier technical universities (IIT Bombay, IIT Kanpur), 2S bridges the gap between high-level boardroom strategy and rigorous, hands-on technical execution. Over two decades, the firm has delivered high-impact initiatives across Fortune 100 corporations, mid-sized enterprises, and state-backed entities in Europe, North America, and Asia.

In 2017, 2S established a dedicated healthcare deep-tech practice, culminating in its proprietary HCare360 healthcare data platform (ISO 27001, 27017, 27018 certified with R&D in Finland). Today, together with its deep-tech affiliate Numantra Technologies and its Nordic commercial agent Bountytek AB, 2S leads the market in transitioning capital-intensive industries (specifically Life Sciences, Medical Devices, and Healthcare Providers) from commoditized hardware/products to high-margin, data-driven cognitive solutions and managed services.
