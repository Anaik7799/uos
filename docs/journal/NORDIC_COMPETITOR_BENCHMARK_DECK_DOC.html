Slide 2: The Four Competitor Archetypes

1. Product Engineering Consultancies (Semcon, Knightec Group, Prevas AB):

  • Strengths: Heavy mechanical design, plastic tooling, CAD/CAM, embedded firmware.
  • Gaps: Software as a Medical Device (SaMD), clinical evaluation, EU AI Act algorithmic auditing, hospital HEOR economics.

2. Pure-Play RA/QA Specialists (Qadvis AB, Key2Compliance, Emergo by UL):

  • Strengths: ISO 13485, MDR technical files, quality system documentation.
  • Gaps: 100% manual billable hour model, lack clinical trial NLP tools, zero commercial sales portal software capability.

3. Full-Service Regional CROs (LINK Medical Research, Syntagon AB):

  • Strengths: Clinical trial site monitoring, bioanalysis, protocol authoring.
  • Gaps: Slow manual patient recruitment, rising monitoring overhead, no AI clinical trial screening software.

4. Tier-1 Strategy & Management Houses (PA Consulting, Sirona Health Solutions):

  • Strengths: Boardroom strategy, public healthcare transformation, M&A due diligence.
  • Gaps: Cost-prohibitive fees (€3,500 - €5,000/day) with near-zero hands-on regulatory technical file execution.
