## 1. The defect this amends

`SC-MIRROR-PROJ-001` §2 mandated a **byte-identical copy** of every canonical contract in each of
four vendor `rules/` surfaces. §4's validation gate checked name-set isomorphism, JSON parse, skill
frontmatter and broken-symlink count — **and nothing that compares mirror bytes to canonical.** The
mandate had no falsifier, so drift was undetectable by the gate that existed to enforce it.

Measured 2026-11-05 over 77 projected files per surface:

| Class | Files | Bytes | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| byte-identical to canonical | 53 | 402,126 | §2 satisfied |
| **divergent from canonical** | **9** | **117,044** | **§2 violated, undetected** |
| no canonical counterpart | 15 | 49,178 | §1 violated: body exists only in mirrors |

In all nine divergences the mirror is **smaller** than canonical and at most one line exists in the
mirror and not in canonical: the signature of stale snapshots, not vendor edits. All four surfaces
agree with each other, so no harness could detect it by peer comparison. Worst case: the
`SC-JOURNAL-v3` text every agent reads is **4,416 B / 62 lines short** of canonical, omitting the
entire 2026-09-13 diagram-check amendment and its open sovereign finding.

## 2. The amendment

§2 is amended from *byte-identical copy* to **generated projection**:

1. A projected entry is a **generated pointer** carrying the contract's name and title and the
   canonical path, with an explicit read-before-acting trigger. Projection-by-name and §4 name-set
   isomorphism are preserved; the body is read on demand from `contracts/rules/`.
2. A **symlink** to the canonical file is an equally conforming projection and is preferred where it
   already exists, because it cannot go stale at all.
3. Projections are **generated by `formal/lean/UosAgentSurfaceGenerator.lean`**, not hand-copied.
4. §4 gains the missing check: `tools/uos-surface-gen --check` compares every projected body against
   canonical and exits non-zero on divergence. This is the falsifier §2 lacked.

**Safety fence.** Only a byte-identical projection may be replaced by a pointer: its bytes provably
survive in canonical, so replacement is lossless. Divergent and canonical-less files are **refused**
and reported — the 9 divergences and 15 orphans remain in place, untouched, pending reconciliation
and promotion respectively.

## 3. Drift from generated output is held, never clobbered

A generated artifact that is hand-edited is **preserved and held**, not overwritten: the edited bytes
are copied to `var/surface-gen/holds/` with a model-amendment proposal, and the hold is cleared only
by folding the change into the Lean model and regenerating. Re-running the generator or overwriting
manually does **not** clear a hold. Proved: `hand_edit_is_never_overwritten`,
`only_model_fold_clears_a_hold`, `write_only_when_nothing_to_lose`.

## 4. Scope extended to skills and superpowers

The same discipline applies to `governance/capability-inventory/skills.toml` and `superpowers.toml`,
which are hand-maintained. The generator owns only a delimited **managed block** inside each file and
never rewrites content outside it. Block replacement is idempotent (`--selftest` check 2).

## 5. Incident during implementation (recorded, not hidden)

While first running `--emit`, one projected entry
(`20260907-1559-risk-prioritization-sop.md`) was a **symlink into `contracts/rules/`**. The write
followed it and **overwrote that canonical contract with a 10-line stub**. The file was restored
byte-exact from `jj @-` (21,245 B) and verified: `jj status` reports it unmodified and zero canonical
files carry the generation marker. A write fence was then added — `resolveFence` refuses any path
whose real path differs from its own, plus a second guard refusing any resolved path under
`contracts/` — with `aliased_path_is_never_written` proved and two self-test checks, one of which
exercises the live symlink. Cause: the generator wrote without checking whether the target was its
own regular file. The same class of mistake (writing through an unexamined symlink) occurred earlier
the same day on the umbrella root, which is why it is recorded here rather than treated as incidental.

## 6. Limits

The amendment is **NOT_ADMITTED** pending sovereign review. A generated pointer is not a summary and
confers no licence to act on a title alone. The 9 §2 violations predate this amendment and are a live
nonconformance against the contract as written. EV ceiling 93; `EV-94..109` `NOT_ADMITTED`; no new EV
number minted. This amendment grants no runtime enforcement and no admission.
