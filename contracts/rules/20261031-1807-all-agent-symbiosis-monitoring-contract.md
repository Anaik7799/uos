   The current operator binding assigns supervision/integration to Codex, bounded eligible execution to Pi, independent review to Claude and peer/sovereign review to AGY. An implementer cannot supply its own independent acceptance; local relay identity must not be mislabeled as another native provider.
2. Mandatory checks belong in authenticated Gleam ingress/execution, not in model memory or advisory hooks. Sa-plan remains sole task authority.
3. Bind every check to the exact agent, candidate, configuration, session, request sequence and monotonic clock domain; bind every coverage leaf to that agent.
4. Require exact service membership and independent function/performance/operation evidence. Missing, stale, future, duplicate, unexpected or mismatched evidence is nonpassing.
5. Heartbeats cannot renew deeper evidence. Reuse only still-valid evidence; invalidate affected evidence on change/failure. Keep due checks fair and single-flight, with spacing and bounded backoff.
6. Report ages, due times, actual probe counters, uncertainty and incomplete service coverage. No invented performance, uptime, model-parallelism or full-symbiosis claims.
7. Confirm worker termination before capacity release. Unknown reaping fences replacement; native/blocking work remains in bounded isolated guardians.
8. Scoped failures require RCA and verified recovery; no automatic unauthorized restart, task completion, inference, refund or grant issuance.
9. Typed Rete facts may advise/veto only after actual consumer wiring. Legacy scalar/sentinel rules do not prove typed integration. Stan/posterior output is advisory; STANAG source admissibility is distinct.
10. Preserve EV93, immutable source history, standalone JJ, Zero-Muda, private credentials and separate production release/state authority.
11. Bind changes to all17 aspects, FMEA raw S/O/Det, all four UCA types, six-domain checklist, paired editable diagrams, 13-section journal and reciprocal KM writeback.
12. Update active canonical mirrors/skills/SOP bindings consistently; preserve imported/history/global sources read-only. Publication proves guidance, not runtime adoption.
