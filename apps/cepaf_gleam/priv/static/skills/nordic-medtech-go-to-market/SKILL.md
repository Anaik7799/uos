# Nordic commercial research

Separate customer segment, country, intended use, buyer, service offer and actual delivery capability. Validate corporate identity, authorization to represent partners, exclusivity, certifications, product capabilities and financial figures from dated primary records. Do not treat Swedish contracting presence as automatic procurement eligibility or claim an uncontested moat.

For legal/regulatory requirements, cite current official sources and applicable scope, effective dates and exceptions; seek competent domain review before relying on conclusions. Never hard-code a region count, mandatory certification, price-quality weighting or pathway as universally current. Distinguish quoted facts, vendor statements, estimates and assumptions.

Use commercial dossiers as leads for verification. Preserve source provenance and alternative market explanations. Offers, prices, savings and timelines are proposals until supported and agreed. Draft outreach only unless sending is explicitly authorized. Use the ACH engine for structured challenges when helpful; market consensus is not clinical safety evidence.
