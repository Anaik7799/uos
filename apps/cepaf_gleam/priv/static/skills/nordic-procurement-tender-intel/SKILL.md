# Tender evidence review

Obtain the actual notice, amendments, deadlines, purchaser, jurisdiction and source URL. Extract mandatory eligibility separately from scored criteria and identify the controlling document for each item. Recheck changing dates and legal requirements against current official sources; report conflicts and unknowns.

Do not assume a default 30/70 price-quality split, mandatory CE marking for every service, or guaranteed access through a local prime. Assess the actual offer, classification, data flows, subcontracting terms and qualifications with competent procurement/legal reviewers. Record potential disqualifiers and a no-bid alternative.

This skill has no background monitor or portal connection. Record what was actually read and when; cached plugin availability is not evidence of access. Prepare bid drafts or questions; submissions and external messages require explicit authorization.
