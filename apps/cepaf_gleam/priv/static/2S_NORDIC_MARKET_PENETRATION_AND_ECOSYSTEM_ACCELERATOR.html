Vector 1: Co-Opetition & Subcontracting Alliances

Strategic Thesis

Major Nordic engineering consultancies—such as Knightec Group (800+ consultants), Semcon (1,200+ consultants), and Prevas AB (900+ consultants)—dominate mechanical engineering, industrial design, embedded systems firmware, and manufacturing automation. However, their leadership teams acknowledge significant capability gaps in:

  • Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) Lifecycle: IEC 62304 architecture for distributed cloud and edge algorithms.
  • EU AI Act & Algorithmic Explainability: Class IIb/III algorithmic auditing and bias mitigation.
  • Clinical Evaluation & HCare360 NLP: Synthesizing real-world clinical evidence and trial matching.
  • Health Economics & Outcomes Research (HEOR): Quantifying ICU bed-day reduction and health economic reimbursement dossiers.

Rather than competing with these entrenched giants for primary account relationships, 2S Consulting positions itself as an elite specialist subcontractor and white-label partner.

Operating Alliance Models

1. White-Label Delivery: Knightec or Semcon contracts a multi-million-euro device overhaul for an OEM (e.g., Getinge or Bactiguard); 2S Consulting delivers the entire IEC 62304 / EU AI Act workstream under the lead partner's banner.

2. Co-Branded Advisory: For high-stakes regulatory remediation, 2S Consulting is introduced as the "Specialist Clinical & Algorithmic Regulatory Affiliate."

3. Subcontracting Economics: 2S bills the primary consultancy at a predictable daily/weekly rate (€1,600 – €2,200/day) or fixed milestone fee, capturing immediate cash flow with zero customer acquisition cost (CAC).

Vector 3: Public Hospital Procurement & Value-Based Healthcare (VBHC) Enablement

The Nordic Procurement Challenge

Nordic public healthcare systems operate under decentralized regional councils (e.g., Region Stockholm, Region Skåne, Västra Götalandsregionen in Sweden; Danske Regioner in Denmark; Regional Health Authorities in Norway).

Public tenders in these jurisdictions are aggressively transitioning from lowest purchase price (LCC) to Value-Based Healthcare (VBHC) and Total Cost of Care (TCC) models. MedTech manufacturers frequently lose tenders because their technical bids lack:

1. Validated economic models demonstrating measurable reductions in length of stay (LOS).

2. Quantifiable reduction in nursing touchpoints and adverse event incidence.

3. Transparent total cost of ownership (TCO) under DaaS subscription structures.

2S Consulting's Tender Enablement Offering

2S Consulting partners directly with mid-market device manufacturers (Tier 2 and Tier 3 targets) to co-author their public procurement response packages:

  • Interactive CFO Value Calculator: Developing dynamic Excel/Web economic models demonstrating net financial return to hospital procurement boards.
  • HEOR Dossier Generation: Packaging retrospective clinical trial data into formal Nordic Health Technology Assessment (HTA) submissions (TLV in Sweden, Behandlingsrådet in Denmark).
  • Outcome Guarantee Structuring: Formulating performance-linked risk-sharing contracts between device vendors and regional hospital authorities.

Vector 4: Notified Body Pre-Assessment Fast-Track ("90-Day Audit-Ready")

The European Certification Bottleneck

The implementation of EU MDR (2017/745) and IVDR (2017/746) has overwhelmed European Notified Bodies (TÜV SÜD, DNV GL Presafe, BSI, Intertek). Device developers wait 14 to 22 months for technical documentation reviews.

A single major non-conformity or deficiency notice delays product launch by an additional 6 to 9 months, costing manufacturers an average of €45,000 to €120,000 per month in lost gross margins and idle burn rate.

The 2S "90-Day Audit-Ready" Rescue Product

2S Consulting deploys a battle-tested, rapid-remediation sprint specifically designed to eliminate submission deficiencies:

1. Days 1–15: Comprehensive Gap Audit: Deep-dive inspection of General Safety and Performance Requirements (GSPR), Clinical Evaluation Report (CER), Risk Management File (ISO 14971), and Post-Market Clinical Follow-up (PMCF).

2. Days 16–60: Document Remediation & Clinical Synthesis: Rewriting deficient CER sections, recalculating statistical power for clinical endpoints, and updating cybersecurity documentation per MDCG 2019-16.

3. Days 61–75: Mock Notified Body Audit: Conducting an adversarial simulation led by seasoned regulatory auditors, pressure-testing internal QA/RA personnel.

4. Days 76–90: Final Dossier Sign-Off: Delivering an audit-sealed technical file ready for first-pass approval.
