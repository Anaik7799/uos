== Yaman · यमन ==
thaat:    Kalyan · कल्याण
time:     evening (sāyaṃkāl)
mood:     serene, devotional twilight raga; the classic first raga taught, all shuddha but a tivra Ma
aroha:    sa स  re रे  ga ग  ^ma ^म  pa प  dha ध  ni नि
avaroha:  ni नि  dha ध  pa प  ^ma ^म  ga ग  re रे  sa स
vadi:     ga · ग
samvadi:  ni · नि

== Bhairav · भैरव ==
thaat:    Bhairav · भैरव
time:     early morning (pratah-kāl)
mood:     solemn, devotional dawn raga invoking Shiva; komal Re and Dha against an otherwise shuddha scale
aroha:    sa स  _re ॒रे  ga ग  ma म  pa प  _dha ॒ध  ni नि
avaroha:  ni नि  _dha ॒ध  pa प  ma म  ga ग  _re ॒रे  sa स
vadi:     dha · ध
samvadi:  re · रे
