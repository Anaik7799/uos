#feature #src-yuque #area-structure #cov-gap

**Coverage**: ✗ gap — a Hermes note may live anywhere under a corpus root; membership of a group is DERIVED from the parent directory, never required

**Use cases**: Force every document to have a home; make "where does this belong?" unanswerable-by-default rather than optional.

**Look & feel (reference)**: Every document sits under exactly one knowledge base; the base is the unit of permission, publication and search scope.

**Navigation cues**: New doc always asks which base; there is no orphan document.
